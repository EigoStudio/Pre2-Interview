Topic: Healthy Fast Food

No. 1: Reading & Question

These days, many people lead busy lives and do not have enough time to cook. Recently, however, stores and restaurants are offering convenient meals with lots of fresh vegetables. By doing so, they help busy people eat healthy food quickly and easily. In the future, more people will probably choose these meals to save time and stay healthy.
Q: According to the passage, how do stores and restaurants help busy people eat healthy food quickly and easily?
Answer: By offering convenient meals with lots of fresh vegetables.

These days, many people use food delivery services.
Do you think using these services is a good idea?
Hint: save time / rainy days / various dishes
1. People can save a lot of time on busy days because they do not have to cook.
2. It is very convenient when the weather is bad or on rainy days.
3. People can easily enjoy various kinds of restaurant food at home.
Hint: expensive / delivery fee / unhealthy
1. Delivery food is often more expensive than eating at restaurants or cooking.
2. People might eat too much fast food, which is not good for their health.
3. Cooking at home is healthier and helps people save money.
