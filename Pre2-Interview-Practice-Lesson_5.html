Topic: Robots in Our Lives

No. 1: Reading & Question

Many people work in factories, offices, and stores. These days, however, companies are starting to use robots instead of humans to do more jobs. By doing so, companies can save time and reduce costs. Some businesses already use robots for tasks like cleaning, delivering food, and assembling products. In the future, more companies will probably rely on robots to do various jobs.
Q: According to the passage, how can companies save time and reduce costs?
Answer: By using robots instead of humans to do more jobs.

No. 4: Your Opinion

These days, many restaurants use robots to serve food.
Do you think this is a good idea?
Hint: fast service / fun for children / staff shortage
1. Robots can deliver food quickly and without making mistakes.
2. It is helpful for restaurants because they often do not have enough staff.
3. Watching robots carry dishes is fun for customers, especially children.
Hint: polite service / trouble / lose jobs
1. I prefer human service because human waiters are friendlier and polite.
2. When robots break down, it can cause problems in the restaurant.
3. Some people might lose their jobs if restaurants use too many robots.

No. 5: Your Opinion

Q: Would you like to have a cleaning robot in your home in the future?

Hint: save time / clean automatically / relax
1. It can clean the floor while I am at school or out of the house.
2. I can save time and have more free time for studying or hobbies.
3. It keeps my room clean every day without much effort.
Hint: expensive / clean by myself / small room
1. Cleaning robots are very expensive to buy and maintain.
2. My room is small and has many things on the floor, so it might not work well.
3. I prefer cleaning by myself because it does not take much time.
