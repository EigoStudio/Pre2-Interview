英検準2級 Interview Practice Lesson 10

Topic: Buying Used Items

No. 1: Reading & Question

These days, buying used goods has become very popular among many people. Many people buy used clothes, books, and games online or at secondhand shops. By doing so, they can save money and help reduce waste. Also, selling items they do not use anymore helps people get some extra money. In the future, more people will likely choose to buy and sell used items.
Q: According to the passage, how can people save money and help reduce waste?
Answer: By buying used clothes, books, and games online or at secondhand shops.

No. 4: Your Opinion

These days, many people use smartphone apps to sell things they do not need.
Do you think using such apps is a good idea?
Hint: make money / reduce trash / easy to use
1. People can get some money by selling things they do not use.
2. It helps reduce trash because other people can reuse those items.
3. Anyone can easily sell items anytime by using their smartphones.
Hint: trouble with other people / pack items / take time
1. Sometimes people have trouble when communicating with buyers.
2. It takes a lot of time to take pictures and send items by post.
3. Some items might get broken or lost during delivery.

No. 5: Your Opinion

Q: Do you often buy used books or games instead of new ones?

Hint: save money / buy more / find old things
1. Used books and games are cheaper, so I can save my pocket money.
2. Because they are cheap, I can buy more books to read.
3. I can sometimes find old books that are not sold in new bookstores.
Hint: prefer clean things / dirty or broken / new items
1. I like buying new items because they are clean and nice.
2. Used things sometimes have scratches or dirty spots.
3. New games and books are in perfect condition and last longer.
