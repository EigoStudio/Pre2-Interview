Topic: Traveling with Family

No. 1: Reading & Question

Traveling with family is a great way to spend time together during vacations. Many families plan trips to enjoy new activities and make good memories. Today, parents often search for popular hotels and travel plans on the Internet before their trips. By doing so, they can find comfortable places to stay and enjoy their trips easily. In the future, more family trips will be planned using the Internet.
Q: According to the passage, how can parents find comfortable places to stay and enjoy their trips easily?
Answer: By searching for popular hotels and travel plans on the Internet before their trips.

No. 4: Your Opinion

These days, many families travel by car instead of taking trains.
Do you think traveling by car is a good idea?
Hint: carry heavy bags / visit many places / talk freely
1. Families can carry heavy bags easily in their cars.
2. They can visit places that are far from train stations.
3. They can talk and listen to music freely without worrying about other people.
Hint: traffic jams / get tired / expensive gasoline
1. Drivers often get caught in heavy traffic jams during holidays.
2. Driving for a long time makes parents very tired.
3. Gas and parking usually cost a lot of money.

No. 5: Your Opinion

Q: Do you like going camping with your family?

Hint: nature / fresh air / cook outside
1. I can enjoy fresh air and beautiful nature outside.
2. It is very fun to cook dinner and eat outside with my family.
3. I can see many stars in the sky at night.
Hint: bugs / uncomfortable / stay at home
1. I do not like insects and sleeping in tents is uncomfortable for me.
2. Preparing tents and cleaning up takes a lot of time.
3. I prefer staying at home and relaxing in my room.
