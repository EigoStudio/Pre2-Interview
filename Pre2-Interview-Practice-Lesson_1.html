Topic: AI at School

No. 1: Reading & Question

Today, many students use AI when they study. Students can ask AI many questions, and by doing so they can save a lot of time because they can get answers quickly. Also, AI can help them learn new things in a fun way. In this way, more students are becoming interested in using technology for their studies.
Q: According to the passage, how can students save a lot of time?
Answer: By asking AI many questions.

No. 4: Your Opinion

Q: Do you think more schools will use AI for English lessons in the future?

Hint: practice speaking / anytime / without being shy
1. Students can practice speaking English with AI anytime they want.
2. Some students are shy, but they can talk to AI without worrying about making mistakes.
3. AI can check students' pronunciation quickly in class.
Hint: fun / feelings / human teachers
1. Talking with human teachers is more fun for students.
2. AI cannot understand students' feelings very well.
3. I think it is better to learn English from real people.

No. 5: Your Opinion

Q: Do you want to use AI when you travel to foreign countries?

Hint: easy to talk / local people / understand signs
1. It is easy to talk to local people using AI devices.
2. AI can help me understand foreign signs and menus quickly.
3. I don't have to carry a heavy dictionary when I travel.
Hint: own words / make friends / study harder
1. I want to speak with my own words to make friends.
2. I want to study English harder by myself without using AI.
3. Talking without AI is more exciting and fun for me.
